1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the minimum standard for how Sahyog Wealth Advisors speaks with its customers on the phone. It protects customers from mis-selling, protects their personal and financial information, and gives every executive one consistent way to open, handle and close a call.

It covers mutual fund SIP registration and changes (top-up, pause, cancellation), lump-sum purchases, redemptions, KYC and account updates, eMandate / NACH registration, portfolio queries, complaints and renewal calls. Every recorded call is scored against this policy by the call analysis system and reviewed by the quality team.

2. Call opening (SOP 1)

  1. Answer every inbound call within three rings. Begin speaking within five seconds of the call connecting.
  2. Use the approved greeting in the customer's preferred language (English, Hindi or Gujarati):
“Good morning / Good afternoon, this is <your name> from Sahyog Wealth Advisors. How may I assist you today?”
  1. On outbound calls, state the purpose of the call in the first 30 seconds and confirm it is a convenient time to speak. If it is not, offer a call-back slot and log it.
  2. Inform the customer that the call is recorded for quality and training purposes.

3. Customer identity verification (SOP 2)

No account-specific information may be shared, and no request may be acted on, until the caller is verified.

  1. Confirm that the call is from the registered mobile number on the folio.
  2. Verify any two of: date of birth, last four characters of PAN, registered email address, or folio number.
  3. If the caller is not the account holder (for example a family member), share only general product information. Account changes need the holder's own verification.
Never ask for an OTP, net-banking or app password, debit-card number, CVV or UPI PIN — under any circumstances. A request for any of these is a zero-tolerance breach (Section 9).

4. Understanding the request (SOP 3)

  1. Let the customer finish explaining. Do not interrupt more than once to clarify.
  2. Paraphrase the request back in one sentence and get confirmation before acting (“So you would like to increase your monthly SIP from ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 from the next instalment — is that right?”).
  3. Check the folio, SIP and mandate status on screen before answering. Do not guess dates or amounts.

5. Product discussion and mandatory disclosures (SOP 4)

Mandatory risk disclaimer. Whenever a scheme, fund performance or expected returns are discussed, the executive must say: “Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.”
  1. Never promise, guarantee or imply assured returns, capital protection or a fixed growth rate. Past performance may be quoted only as past performance.
  2. Recommend a scheme only after confirming the customer's investment goal, horizon and risk appetite as recorded in the risk profile. If no risk profile exists, offer to complete it before recommending.
  3. Disclose exit loads, lock-in periods (for ELSS: three years) and applicable charges before confirming any purchase or redemption.
  4. Do not compare Sahyog-distributed schemes unfavourably with other distributors or make claims that cannot be supported by the scheme documents.

7. Hold and transfer (SOP 6)

  1. Ask the customer's permission before placing the call on hold and tell them why.
  2. A hold must not exceed two minutes. Return to the line at least every 60 seconds if more time is needed.
  3. Thank the customer for holding when you return.
  4. Transfers must be warm: brief the receiving executive before connecting, so the customer does not have to repeat the request.

8. Complaint handling and escalation

  1. Acknowledge the concern and apologise for the inconvenience, without admitting fault before checking the facts.
  2. Log a complaint ticket during the call and share the ticket number with the customer before closing.
  3. Resolve, or update the customer, within 48 working hours. Unresolved complaints move to Level 2 after three working days.
  4. A customer who asks for a supervisor must be transferred or called back by a team leader within four working hours.

9. Zero-tolerance practices

Any of the following leads to immediate escalation to the Compliance team and disciplinary action, regardless of the overall call score:

  • Promising guaranteed or assured returns, or capital protection, on any mutual fund scheme.
  • Asking for an OTP, password, PIN, CVV or full card number.
  • Executing a transaction without verifying the customer's identity.
  • Sharing one customer's information with another person.
  • Abusive, rude or threatening language, or disconnecting a customer deliberately.
  • Recommending a scheme that clearly does not match the customer's recorded risk profile in order to meet a target.

10. Call closure (SOP 7)

  1. Summarise what was done and what happens next, including any timeline and reference number.
  2. Ask: “Is there anything else I can help you with today?”
  3. For every resolved call, offer the post-call feedback IVR: “You will now be transferred to a short feedback survey — it takes less than a minute.”
  4. Close with the approved line: “Thank you for calling Sahyog Wealth Advisors. Have a good day.”

12. Data privacy and call recordings

  1. Customer data may be viewed only for the request in hand. Screens must be locked when a desk is left unattended.
  2. Do not note customer details on paper or personal devices.
  3. Call recordings are retained for eight years and accessed only by Quality, Compliance and authorised team leaders.
